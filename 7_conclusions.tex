\section{Conclusions}
In conclusion, this study offers a discussion of theoretical
concepts combined with rigorous
statistical data analysis of the
factors influencing license choices in open source software
projects. Key trends that shape the
licensing landscape include the impact of project maturity, team
composition, activity levels, and programming languages. 
While permissive licenses dominate many areas
of the open source ecosystem, particularly in newer and more dynamic
projects, a significant and often underappreciated
presence of copyleft and weak copyleft licenses exists in older
and more complex projects. The overhelming prevalence of unlicensed
code may pose risk and prevent full potential for innovation by building on this
abundant but legaly uncertain foundation.

continues to evolve, it is crucial to foster an environment where
legal clarity, sustainability, and community values guide the
selection of licenses, ensuring the long-term health and success of
open source projects.
